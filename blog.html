Complete Guide to Schengen Visa Application from India 2026

Planning a Europe trip? A Schengen visa allows you to travel across 27 European countries with a single visa. Here is everything you need to know before applying from India.

What is a Schengen Visa?

A Schengen visa is a short-stay visa that permits entry into any of the 27 Schengen Area member countries for up to 90 days within any 180-day period. Countries like France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Switzerland, Netherlands, and more are part of this zone.

Types of Schengen Visa

  • Tourist/Visitor Visa (Type C): For holidays, sightseeing, or visiting family
  • Business Visa: For meetings, conferences, or trade visits
  • Transit Visa (Type A/B): For passing through a Schengen airport
  • Student Visa: For short-term courses or workshops

Which Embassy to Apply At?

You must apply at the embassy of your main destination country — the country where you will spend the most nights. If all stays are equal, apply at the country of first entry.

📌 Tip: France, Germany, and Italy are the most common Schengen embassies applied through from India. France has consulates in Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Chennai, and Hyderabad.

Documents Required

  • Valid passport (at least 6 months validity, 2 blank pages)
  • Completed Schengen visa application form
  • 2 recent passport-size photographs (35x45mm, white background)
  • Travel itinerary with confirmed flight bookings
  • Hotel bookings or accommodation proof for all nights
  • Travel insurance (minimum €30,000 medical coverage)
  • Last 3 months bank statements (minimum balance ₹2.5 lakh recommended)
  • Income tax returns (last 2 years)
  • Employment proof: salary slips / business registration / NOC from employer
  • Cover letter explaining purpose of visit

Processing Time

Schengen visa processing typically takes 10–15 working days. During peak season (June–September), it can take up to 30 days. Apply at least 6–8 weeks before your travel date.

Common Reasons for Rejection

  • Insufficient bank balance or irregular financial records
  • Weak ties to home country (no stable job, property, or family)
  • Incomplete or inconsistent documents
  • Applying too close to travel date
  • Previous visa rejections not disclosed

Japan Visa 2026: Documents Required & Step-by-Step Process

Japan has become one of the top dream destinations for Indian tourists. The country does not offer an e-Visa — you must apply at the Japanese Consulate or through an authorized travel agent. Here is the complete process.

Types of Japan Visa

  • Tourist Visa (Single Entry): 15 days, most common
  • Tourist Visa (Double Entry): For two trips within the validity period
  • Multiple Entry Visa: For frequent travellers (requires previous Japan visit)

Documents Required

  • Original passport (6+ months validity, at least 2 blank pages)
  • Visa application form (downloaded from Japanese Consulate website)
  • Passport-size photo (45mm x 45mm, white background, taken within 6 months)
  • Day-wise travel itinerary covering all cities/hotels
  • Return flight tickets (confirmed)
  • Hotel bookings for all nights
  • Bank statement (last 6 months, minimum balance ₹3–4 lakh recommended)
  • Income Tax Returns (last 2 years)
  • Employment certificate / business registration / student ID
  • Leave approval letter from employer (for salaried applicants)

📌 Note (2026): Japan now requires all applications to be submitted through authorized travel agents or consulate directly. Walk-in applications have been discontinued in Chennai and Hyderabad since March 2026.

Singapore Visa from India: Requirements, Fees & Processing Time

Singapore is a favourite short-trip destination for Indians. The Singapore Tourist Visa (also called an Electronic Visa) is processed online and usually approved within 3–5 working days.

Who Needs a Visa?

Indian passport holders require a visa to visit Singapore. The visa is issued as an e-Visa (no sticker) and is linked electronically to your passport.

Types of Singapore Visa

  • Single Entry Visa: Valid for 30 days per entry, 2-year validity
  • Multiple Entry Visa: Multiple visits within 2 years, 30 days each stay

Documents Required

  • Passport (valid for at least 6 months beyond date of departure)
  • Coloured passport-size photograph (white background)
  • Confirmed return flight tickets
  • Hotel bookings for entire stay
  • Bank statement – last 3 months (recommended balance ₹1.5 lakh+)
  • Proof of employment / business / student status

Thailand Visa-Free for Indians 2026: What You Need to Carry

Great news for Indian travellers — Thailand has extended visa-free entry for Indian passport holders for up to 60 days. However, there are conditions and documents you must carry to avoid being denied boarding or entry.

Thailand Visa-Free Policy (2026)

As of 2026, Indian citizens can visit Thailand without a visa for up to 60 days per visit. You may also register for the TDAC (Tourist Digital Arrival Card) online before travel.

📌 Important: Although no visa is required, immigration officers may still ask for proof of sufficient funds, return tickets, and hotel bookings. Always carry these documents.

Documents to Carry (Even Without Visa)

  • Valid passport (6+ months validity from date of entry)
  • Confirmed return flight ticket
  • Hotel bookings for all nights of stay
  • Proof of sufficient funds: Bank statement or cash (recommended THB 10,000 per person)
  • TDAC registration confirmation (if applicable)

What is TDAC?

The Tourist Digital Arrival Card is Thailand's digital immigration form. Travellers are encouraged to fill it online before departure to speed up the immigration process at Thai airports. VacationPe assists with TDAC registration starting at ₹250 per person.

Things to Know Before Visiting Thailand

  • You cannot extend a visa-free stay beyond 60 days
  • Back-to-back visa-free entries may be questioned by immigration
  • Carry a printed copy of hotel bookings and tickets at immigration
  • The legal age for alcohol consumption in Thailand is 20 years
  • Disrespecting the Thai royal family is a serious criminal offence

Best Places to Visit in Thailand

  • Bangkok: Grand Palace, Chatuchak Market, street food
  • Phuket: Patong Beach, Phi Phi Islands, snorkelling
  • Chiang Mai: Elephant sanctuaries, temples, night bazaar
  • Krabi: Railay Beach, rock climbing, boat tours

US Tourist Visa (B1/B2): Interview Tips & Common Rejection Reasons

Getting a US tourist visa as an Indian national involves a thorough process — from filling the DS-160 form to attending an interview at the US Consulate. Here is a complete guide to maximize your chances of approval.

What is a B1/B2 Visa?

The B-1/B-2 visa is a combined non-immigrant visa for tourism (B-2) and business (B-1) visits to the United States. It is typically issued for 10 years with multiple entry privileges, allowing each stay of up to 6 months.

Step-by-Step Application Process

  1. Pay the MRV (Machine Readable Visa) fee of $185 (approximately ₹15,400)
  2. Fill DS-160 online form on the US Consulate website
  3. Create a profile on the US Visa appointment portal (ustraveldocs.com)
  4. Schedule visa interview at nearest US Consulate (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad)
  5. Attend the interview with all required documents
  6. If approved, passport is returned with visa stamp within 3–10 working days

Documents Required for Interview

  • Valid passport + all old passports
  • DS-160 confirmation page
  • Appointment confirmation letter
  • MRV fee payment receipt
  • Passport-size photograph as per US specifications
  • Proof of financial stability: Bank statements, ITR, salary slips
  • Proof of strong ties to India: property documents, family, employment letter
  • Travel itinerary (not mandatory but recommended)

US Visa Interview Tips

  • Be confident and give short, direct answers — avoid over-explaining
  • Know your travel purpose clearly; rehearse but do not memorize scripted answers
  • Show strong reasons to return to India (job, family, property, business)
  • Never lie or exaggerate — consular officers are highly experienced
  • Dress formally and arrive early at the consulate
  • Carry original documents, not just photocopies

Common Reasons for US Visa Rejection

ReasonHow to Address It
