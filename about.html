Who We Are

VacationPe – Your Trusted Travel Partner

Founded in 2019 by Bajrang Singh Shekhawat, VacationPe is a Jaipur-based travel company backed by over 13 years of industry expertise. We believe every journey should be as beautiful as the destination itself.

We handle everything — from customized domestic & international holiday packages to flight bookings, hotel reservations, travel insurance, forex, visa assistance, and expert consultation. One call, and your dream vacation is in motion.

Our mission is to turn your travel dreams into reality by offering carefully designed holiday experiences that you will cherish for a lifetime. Whether it is a family vacation, honeymoon, celebration trip, or international holiday, our team ensures that every journey is smooth, memorable, and perfectly planned.
