About Us

Discover The World With VacationPe

At VacationPe, we believe that travel is more than just visiting places — it is about creating unforgettable memories. Our mission is to turn your travel dreams into reality by offering carefully designed holiday experiences that you will cherish for a lifetime.

We specialize in tailor-made travel packages for every traveler — family vacations, honeymoons, celebration trips, or international holidays. Every journey is smooth, memorable, and perfectly planned.

Founder & Managing Partner

Bajrang Singh Shekhawat

Bajrang Singh Shekhawat is the Founder and Managing Partner of VacationPe, bringing over 13 years of experience in tourism and travel.

Previously worked with MakeMyTrip, Musafir.com and Ritco Travels. Holds a BA in Travel & Tourism and an IATA Foundation Diploma.

Active member of ADTOI and BNI Jaipur.

His vision is to provide travelers with unique, seamless, and unforgettable journeys while maintaining the highest standards of service and satisfaction.
