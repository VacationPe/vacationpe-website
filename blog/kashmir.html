Kashmir Travel Guide 2026

Heaven on Earth — Dal Lake, Gulmarg, Pahalgam & Beyond

Kashmir — the crown jewel of India — needs no introduction. From snow-capped Himalayan peaks and flower-filled valleys to serene Dal Lake houseboats and world-class ski slopes, Kashmir is truly Heaven on Earth. Whether you're a honeymooner, adventure seeker, or family traveller, Kashmir delivers an experience unlike anywhere else in India.

Top Places to Visit in Kashmir

🛶 Srinagar — The Summer Capital

Srinagar is the heart of Kashmir tourism, famous for its iconic Dal Lake where you can stay in traditional wooden houseboats (shikaras), visit the floating vegetable market at dawn, and explore Mughal gardens like Shalimar Bagh and Nishat Bagh.

  • Dal Lake Shikara ride: Morning & sunset rides — a must-do
  • Mughal Gardens: Shalimar Bagh, Nishat Bagh, Chashme Shahi
  • Hazratbal Shrine on the banks of Dal Lake
  • Lal Chowk for shopping — Pashmina, dry fruits, saffron, Kashmiri carpets

⛷️ Gulmarg — Ski Capital of India

Gulmarg, at 2,650 metres altitude, is India's premier ski destination and home to one of the world's highest gondola cable cars. In winter it transforms into a snowy paradise; in summer it becomes a lush green meadow with stunning Himalayan views.

📌 2026 Tip: Gulmarg Gondola Phase 2 (Apharwat Peak, 4,200m) offers panoramic views of Nanga Parbat. Book gondola tickets online in advance during peak season — queues can be 2–3 hours long.

🌸 Pahalgam — Valley of Shepherds

Pahalgam is famous for the Amarnath Yatra base camp, the Lidder River, and stunning meadows like Betaab Valley and Aru Valley. It's also the most popular honeymoon destination in Kashmir.

  • Betaab Valley: Named after the Bollywood film — lush green with snow-capped mountains
  • Chandanwari: Starting point of Amarnath Yatra, famous for snow sledging
  • Baisaran (Mini Switzerland): Accessible only by horse — breathtaking views

🏔️ Sonamarg — Meadow of Gold

Sonamarg sits at 2,800m and serves as the gateway to Ladakh via the Zoji La pass. The Thajiwas Glacier is the main attraction — a short trek from the main road where you can walk on glacier ice.

🌺 Yusmarg & Doodhpathri — Hidden Gems

Lesser-visited but equally beautiful, these meadows offer peace, pony rides, and stunning scenery without the tourist crowds of Gulmarg or Pahalgam.

Best Time to Visit Kashmir

SeasonMonthsExperience
❄️ WinterDecember – FebruaryHeavy snowfall, skiing in Gulmarg, frozen Dal Lake
🌸 Spring (Best)March – MayCherry blossoms, tulip festival, pleasant weather
☀️ SummerJune – AugustPeak season, all places open, Amarnath Yatra
🍂 AutumnSeptember – NovemberChinar trees turn golden, fewer crowds, lower prices
🌷 Don't miss: The Tulip Garden in Srinagar (Asia's largest tulip garden) blooms in late March – mid April. It's free to enter and absolutely spectacular.

How to Reach Kashmir

  • By Air: Srinagar International Airport has direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Jaipur, Bengaluru, Kolkata. Flight time from Jaipur is about 1.5 hours.
  • By Train + Road: Train to Jammu, then road journey to Srinagar (about 8–9 hours via NH44 through Jawahar Tunnel)
  • By Road: Delhi to Srinagar is approximately 850 km (16–18 hours drive)

Documents Required (Indian Citizens)

  • Valid Government ID (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID)
  • No special permit required for Indian citizens to visit Kashmir Valley
  • For restricted areas (some near Gurez, Lolab Valley) — Inner Line Permit may be needed

Ideal Trip Duration

  • 4–5 Days: Srinagar + Gulmarg + Pahalgam (best for first-time visitors)
  • 6–7 Days: Add Sonamarg and Doodhpathri
  • 8–10 Days: Full Kashmir + extension to Ladakh via Kargil
